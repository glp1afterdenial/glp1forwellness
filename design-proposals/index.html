Design Competition — glp1forwellness.com

4 Opus design agents · 4 perspectives · judged on visual quality, SEO/E-E-A-T signals, conversion, mobile, and brief compliance. Click any screenshot to expand full-length.

🏛️ Verdict

🥇
Conversion — NerdWallet-style navy/white/emerald WINNER

Most complete and most polished. The TL;DR "Quick Answer" box near the top is exactly what featured snippets and AI answer engines extract. Hero stat card ("20% fewer heart attacks", "3.1 years younger") gives instant credibility; the dark $129/mo price-anchor bar vs struck-through $1,000+ is the single strongest revenue element of any proposal. Affiliate disclosure bar above the header is an FTC/E-E-A-T win Google's raters explicitly look for. Evidence badges (FDA-APPROVED / RCT DATA / EMERGING) on every condition card. Mobile rendering is flawless — full-width tap targets, cards stack cleanly, sticky CTA.

🥈
Editorial — Serif medical-journal, navy + ivory

The strongest trust design. Evidence-grade badge taxonomy with an on-page legend (green = FDA-approved, amber = strong cohort, purple = observational) is a genuinely original E-E-A-T device, and the article metadata bar (published date · 8 min read · 6 studies cited) mirrors what quality raters reward. Article typography is the most readable of the four. Loses to Conversion only on monetization: no price anchoring, weaker CTAs, and the serif-heavy look converts trust better than it converts clicks.

🥉
Minimal — gov.uk/Stripe-docs restraint, system fonts, zero JS

Technically excellent — fastest possible LCP, AAA contrast, semantic HTML, <details> FAQs. But visually it's the least differentiated: white cards on gray with modest hierarchy. It's a very good "engineering baseline," not the redesign you asked for.

4th
Brand — "Clinical Midnight" dark mode + electric teal BROKE BRIEF

The most visually striking, but it ignored the light-mode-only constraint. Dark UI is also the wrong instinct for a health site skewing 45+ — reads as crypto/biohacker, not medical authority — and the bento-grid hero rendered sparse/misaligned. Worth stealing: the gradient-mesh hero technique and pill-nav treatment, in a light palette.

Recommendation: Ship Conversion as the base. Graft on two Editorial elements: (1) the evidence-grade badge legend on the homepage, and (2) the article metadata bar ("Published · X min read · N studies cited"). That combo maximizes both the SEO/E-E-A-T story and affiliate revenue while staying light-mode.
