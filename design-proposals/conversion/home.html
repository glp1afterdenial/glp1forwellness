Why GLP-1s Help With More Than Weight

How GLP-1s work beyond appetite

GLP-1 receptor agonists (like Ozempic, Wegovy, and Zepbound) do more than reduce appetite. They suppress inflammatory pathways (NF-kB), reduce cytokines (TNF-alpha, IL-6), protect kidneys, and have neuroprotective effects. These mechanisms help explain why GLP-1s show benefits for autoimmune conditions, liver disease, sleep apnea, joint pain, and mental health.

The access problem
  • ✕ Insurance rarely covers GLP-1s for conditions beyond diabetes and obesity
