## How Oak Loves You Works {#how-it-works}

Oak keeps the signup process simple. There's no appointment to schedule and no video call required. Here's what happens:

### Step 1: Take the Quiz (5 minutes)

When you click through to Oak, you enter your email and take a short health quiz. It asks:

- **Your height and weight** — to calculate your BMI
- **Health conditions** — diabetes, high blood pressure, sleep apnea, PCOS, etc.
- **Current medications** — to check for interactions
- **Allergies** — important for compounding
- **Your goals** — weight loss, metabolic health, etc.

The quiz takes about 5 minutes. It's not a sales funnel — it's the actual medical intake that a licensed provider reviews to decide whether GLP-1 medication is appropriate for you.

### Step 2: Choose Your Treatment and Plan

After the quiz, you choose between **tirzepatide** (Oak's recommended option) or **semaglutide** (most affordable), and pick a plan length. Both medications include **Vitamin B12**. You also have the option to choose Glycine instead. Then you select a plan — 12-month, 6-month, 3-month, or month-to-month.

You're **only charged if a provider approves your treatment**. If they determine GLP-1 medication isn't right for you, you get a **full refund**.

### Step 3: Provider Reviews Your Intake

A licensed provider reviews your quiz answers and health history **right after checkout**. You have 24/7 access to the care team if you have questions before, during, or after the review. You can reach them by phone at (435) 244-7757, text, or email.

### Step 4: Medication Ships Free in 1-2 Days

Once approved, your medication ships **free in 1-2 business days with tracking** from a state-licensed US 503A compounding pharmacy. It arrives pre-mixed and ready to use with injection supplies and instructions.
