## How Telehealth Peptide Therapy Works {#how-it-works}

All three platforms follow the same basic model:

1. **Online intake** — health questionnaire covering your medical history, current medications, goals, and any contraindications
2. **Provider review** — a licensed clinician evaluates your case. They approve, suggest adjustments, or decline
3. **Prescription fills** — at a licensed US 503A compounding pharmacy, tested for purity and potency
4. **Medication ships** — pre-mixed, pre-dosed, to your door (typically 2-day shipping)
5. **Ongoing access** — message your provider to adjust dosing, report side effects, or add therapies

No clinic visits. No waiting rooms. No insurance paperwork. The provider evaluation is included in the monthly price.

<div class="callout callout--amber">

**What "compounded" means:** These peptides are made by licensed US compounding pharmacies — not mass-manufactured by pharma companies. The pharmacy compounds each prescription individually under FDA guidelines (cGMP). The active ingredients are the same; the difference is that compounded versions aren't FDA-approved as finished products, which is why they cost dramatically less. This is the same compounding model used for decades across medicine.

</div>

## Frequently Asked Questions {#faq}

<div class="callout">

**Are these platforms legitimate?**

Yes. All three platforms work with licensed US compounding pharmacies and employ licensed clinicians (MDs, DOs, NPs, PAs) who review patient intakes and write prescriptions. This is standard telehealth medicine — the same regulatory framework that covers telehealth prescriptions for any other medication. It's fundamentally different from buying "research peptides" online.

</div>

<div class="callout">

**Do I need a specific BMI or diagnosis to get peptide therapy?**

For GLP-1 medications, most platforms require BMI 27+ with a comorbidity or BMI 30+. For sermorelin, NAD+, and PT-141, there's no BMI requirement — you need a legitimate clinical reason (age-related GH decline, fatigue, sexual health concern) and pass the provider's health screening. Contraindications (active cancer, pregnancy, pituitary tumors) will disqualify you regardless.

</div>

<div class="callout">

**Can I switch peptides or add one later?**

Yes. Most platforms let you adjust your program — add NAD+ to sermorelin, switch from injection to oral, or start GLP-1 microdosing alongside an existing peptide. Bodybuilding Health+ makes this easiest with their all-in-one menu. With other platforms, you may need a separate subscription for each peptide.

</div>

<div class="callout">

**Is peptide therapy covered by insurance?**

Generally no — not for wellness, anti-aging, or body composition purposes. GLP-1 medications may be covered if prescribed for an FDA-approved indication (obesity, diabetes, sleep apnea, MASH, CKD). Sermorelin, NAD+, and PT-141 for wellness are cash-pay. Some platforms accept HSA/FSA cards, which lets you use pre-tax dollars.

</div>

<div class="callout">

**What's the difference between these platforms and an anti-aging clinic?**

The medication is the same — compounded peptides from licensed pharmacies. The difference is overhead. Anti-aging clinics charge for office visits, blood draws, fancy waiting rooms, and markup. Telehealth eliminates that overhead and passes the savings to you. A $500-1,500/month clinic program becomes $99-209/month through telehealth. The tradeoff: you don't get in-person exams or on-site blood draws (though most platforms can order labs through Quest or Labcorp if needed).

</div>
