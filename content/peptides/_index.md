<div class="peptide-intro">

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that act as **signaling molecules** — they tell cells to release growth hormone, regulate appetite, repair tissue, or manage inflammation. Your body makes hundreds of them naturally. **Semaglutide and tirzepatide — the GLP-1 drugs — are peptides.**

Therapeutic peptides are lab-made versions of these signals. **Sermorelin** prompts your pituitary to release more growth hormone. **NAD+** (technically a coenzyme, not a peptide) supports cellular energy and repair. They're usually offered on the same telehealth platforms, so we cover them together.

The appeal is precision. The catch is that **quality, dosing, and sourcing matter enormously.**
